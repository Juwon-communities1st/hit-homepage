Herts Inclusive Theatre

Inclusive theatre, made together in Hertfordshire

A stage for everyone, for more than twenty years.

Disabled and non-disabled children, young people and adults make drama, performances and films side by side with professional theatre-makers. Join a group, bring us to your school or workplace, or come and see the work.

Who we are

Our mission

Registered charity 1180955
Based at Trestle Arts Base, St Albans

We use theatre to break down barriers about disability. Everyone is encouraged, valued and supported, and everyone has their part to play.

Over 1,000 people a year take part in our weekly groups, holiday workshops, community projects, productions and the annual Enrich Festival.
